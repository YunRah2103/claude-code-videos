# Yunus (character model)

Pixel-art character for reuse across videos. Style matches the TikTok explainer reference: chunky pixels, dark navy outline, flat colors, plain white background.

- `yunus.png`: transparent, 12x scale (432x672), use this in videos
- `yunus_1x.png`: raw 36x56 pixels, scale with nearest-neighbor only
- `yunus_preview.png`: same on a white background
- `generate.py`: regenerates all three (`python3 generate.py`, needs Pillow)

Look: dark wavy hair with side-swept fringe, silver wire-rim glasses, warm medium-brown skin, small smile, plain white t-shirt, baggy jeans, white shoes.

Change colors in the palette at the top of `generate.py` (`SK`/`SH` skin, `GL` glasses, `JN`/`JD`/`JL` jeans).
