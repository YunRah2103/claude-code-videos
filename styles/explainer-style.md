# Explainer video style guide

Derived from the reference video "Gorillas explain Tax Evasion" (1080x1920, 60fps, ~61s).

- **Canvas:** vertical 1080x1920, plain white background.
- **Characters:** chunky pixel-art sprites with a dark outline, centred, roughly the middle third of the frame. Same character is reused in different outfits for different roles (e.g. worker, government, agent). Our character: `characters/main-guy`.
- **Props:** pixel-art objects (documents, money piles, cars, logos) pop in beside the character to illustrate the line being spoken. A full pixel-art scene backdrop is used for the punchline/ending.
- **Captions:** bold white text with a thick black outline, placed under the sprite, 1-3 words at a time, changing in sync with the voiceover.
- **Pacing:** a new visual or caption roughly every 1-3 seconds; sprite stays on screen while props and captions swap.
- **Voiceover:** continuous narration drives all timing; captions follow the words.
