# Toyota Mirai: hydrogen in 5 minutes

You can fill this car up in 5 minutes. So why can't you find anywhere to do it? Toyota's Mirai fills up with hydrogen in about 5 minutes. Toyota claims it can drive up to 402 miles. But in 2023, there were only 59 hydrogen stations in the US, mostly in California. Would you drive one if your nearest station was a long way off?
