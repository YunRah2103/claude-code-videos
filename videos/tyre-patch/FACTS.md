| "All four together: barely a sheet of paper" | 4 x ~156-160 cm^2 = ~625-640 cm^2; A4 = 21.0 x 29.7 cm = 624 cm^2. The four drawn patches tile an A4 sheet exactly. | Arithmetic; ISO 216 |
