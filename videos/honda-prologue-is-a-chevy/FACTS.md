# Honda Prologue is a Chevy — fact sheet

Every claim spoken or shown, as used. Facts come from the session brief (2026-11-19); they were not re-checked against
primary sources in this session. Wording was kept at or below the strength of the brief.

Softened: "a Chevy factory" is said as "GM's Ramos Arizpe plant ... same plant as the Blazer EV" (GM, not Chevrolet,
owns the plant). "First major EV" is spoken as "first big EV". "Is it still a Honda?" is left to the viewer.

Photos: 2024 Prologue Touring (Kevauto) and 2024 Blazer EV RS (Elise240SX), both front-left three-quarter, Wikimedia
Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0 (`CREDITS.txt` next to the MP4). The brief's starting file "2024 Honda Prologue Elite AWD (United
States) front view.png" was not used: an auto-show sign stand covers its front wheel, so it could not be cut out cleanly;
it also faces the other way from the Blazer EV shot. No spec is read from the photos. The six background gags (shark fin,
mime, UFO, pigeon inspector, disco dancer, snowman in a hot tub) are cartoon jokes, not claims.
