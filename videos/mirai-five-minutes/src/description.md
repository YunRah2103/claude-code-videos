# You can fill it in five minutes. So where?

You can fill this car up in five minutes. So why can't you find anywhere to do it? Toyota's Mirai runs on hydrogen, and it refuels in about five minutes. But in 2023, there were only 59 hydrogen stations in the whole US. Most of them in California. Would you drive one, if your nearest station was a long way off?

Tell me in the comments: would you?

The station map is illustrative: the dots show the 2023 total and that most were in California, not exact station locations.
