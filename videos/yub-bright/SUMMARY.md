# Yub Cars (@yub_cars): three bright low-poly shorts

Each short is 1080x1920 at 30 fps and under 25 s. Each has one Gemini TTS narrator (voice Charon, one call per video), with the first word at 0.30 s. A large hook card shows from frame 0, and the captions are large and in a fixed position.

Background music in all three is "Hot Swing" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). A synthesised rock backbeat (kick and snare, locked to the track's 144 BPM grid) is layered on top. The music sits about 4 dB under the voice, ducks lightly while the narrator talks, and has a 1.2–4 kHz dip so the voice stays clear.

**Required credit for each upload description:** Music: "Hot Swing" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License, http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The main character (Yub) is a 3D low-poly version of the skit character. He has brown skin, dark hair with a middle part, silver square glasses, a navy blazer over an open white shirt, charcoal trousers and white sneakers. He never speaks; he reacts.

## 3. gm_carplay.mp4 (24.8 s)

**Final narration**
> GM wants your car to forget your iPhone. Plug in your phone... nothing. In 2023, GM began dropping CarPlay and Android Auto from new EVs. Future GM cars follow, around 2028. GM calls it a better experience. Your phone? No thanks. Yet nearly nine in ten shoppers value it, and forty-six percent call it a must-have. Nearly half of shoppers say they must have it. GM says: how about no. Would you buy a car without CarPlay? Yes, or no?

**Facts used and sources**
| Fact | Source | Check |
|---|---|---|
| GM began dropping CarPlay and Android Auto from its EVs in 2023 | https://www.kbb.com/car-news/gm-will-abandon-apple-carplay-android-auto/ | Verified: "The company eliminated them from electric cars in 2023" |
| GM will remove them from its future vehicles, with the change tied to a new computing platform in 2028 | https://www.automotiveworld.com/news/gm-confirms-it-will-drop-apple-carplay-starting-in-2028/ | Verified: "remove Apple CarPlay and Android Auto support from all of its future vehicles" … "new centralised computing architecture in 2028" |
| GM describes the change as moving to "a better, more deeply integrated experience"; gas models keep CarPlay for the "foreseeable future"; existing cars are not changed | https://www.macrumors.com/2025/10/22/gm-phasing-out-carplay-all-cars/ | Verified (all three). The video says "future GM cars", never that it has been removed from every car today |
| Nearly 9 in 10 shoppers value smartphone integration; 46% call it a must-have | https://www.cars.com/articles/gm-to-remove-apple-carplay-android-auto-from-future-vehicles-517813/ | Verified: "nearly 9 in 10 shoppers think it's valuable", "46% consider smartphone integration a must-have feature" |

**Changed or left out:**
- "Most new EVs": KBB says GM eliminated them "from electric cars in 2023" and does not say "most". The narration says GM "began dropping" them from new EVs.
- "GM's CEO said…": the 2028 statement is attributed to GM (spokesperson Malorie Lucich), not to Mary Barra. Barra is quoted only on customer feedback. The narration therefore says "GM", not "GM's CEO".
- "To offer a better experience": GM's statement is about moving to "a better, more deeply integrated experience". The narration says "GM calls it a better experience" rather than presenting it as GM's stated reason.

"No thanks" on the car's screen and the giant NO button are jokes, not GM statements.

**Background oddity (never mentioned):** an astronaut in a full spacesuit lounging on a striped deck chair under a beach umbrella on the dealership forecourt.
