# seatbelt-fight — art direction v2 ("Dusk Street 3D")

1. **Palette:** dusk city — violet buildings with warm amber / pale cyan emissive windows, purple-rose fog, slate road with cream lane dashes; hero in his own colours (brown skin, dark hair, grey glasses as in the sprite, white tee, black shorts, white/black trainers), opponent solid dark red, unbadged teal hatchback, amber seatbelt.
2. **Shapes:** solid flat-shaded 3D limb figures (capsule limbs, rounded torso, sphere heads), low-detail box buildings, a simple car with a removable near door for the cutaway, striped concrete barrier; clean 2D diagram overlays for the explainer.
3. **Lighting:** cool sky hemisphere + low warm key from the left + cyan rim from behind, a warm practical near the fight and a soft fill in the cabin; one impact flash per big hit only.
4. **Camera:** real perspective camera on a spherical rig — slow orbit and push-ins in the fight with a few wide/close cuts, tracking follow on the knock-back and dive, ride-along in the car, wide crash, push-in to the locked belt at the freeze, slow living orbit through the explainer, whip pan back to the street, settle to the opening wide for the loop.
5. **Finish:** depth of field (city layer rendered separately and blurred), fog falloff, 4-sub-frame motion blur (180° shutter), vignette and a fixed (non-flickering) grain; no particles, rings or speed lines.
