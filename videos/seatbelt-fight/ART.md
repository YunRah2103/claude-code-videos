# seatbelt-fight — art direction ("Dusk Street Ink")

1. **Palette:** dusk city street — indigo-to-amber sky (#1a1d48 → #d4687a → #ffcf8a), blurred skyline with lit windows, streetlights, blank neon sign shapes (no text, no brands), wet road reflections; base ink #0d1424 with one warm key (sodium amber #ffb347) and one cool kicker (cyan #5fe3ff); rival in hot vermilion (#ff4a3d), our guy in his own colours (brown skin, dark hair, white tee, black shorts, white trainers); diagram parts in clean cream/amber/cyan.
2. **Shapes:** stickman-proportioned fighters (round heads, thin round-capped limbs, no faces on the rival) — our guy keeps his recognisable traits: messy dark hair, grey square glasses, white tee, black shorts, white/black trainers; diagram drawn as flat rounded vector parts, no outlines heavier than 4 px.
3. **Lighting:** flat fills plus a single warm rim stroke on the light side of every limb, soft ground shadow, bokeh car-park lights; impacts add a white flash frame, radial burst and a short amber bloom spike.
4. **Camera:** hard cuts and impact punch-ins between wide, low-angle and extreme close-ups during the fight; then one continuous move in the same street: shockwave slide, pan left as an unbadged teal car skids in and stops abruptly right behind him, push into the driver's locked belt (freeze), pull back as he steps in and turns to camera; after the explainer the car reverses away and the camera eases back to the opening wide for the loop.
5. **Finish:** soft blurred backdrop (depth), gentle bloom, vignette, fine animated film grain, warm/cool split grade; smear trails on fast limbs, speed lines on dashes, no logos or brand marks anywhere.
