# seatbelt-fight — art direction v4 ("Dusk Street, flat 2D")

1. **Palette:** dusk city street — indigo-to-peach sky, violet skyline, plum shopfronts with warm lit windows and plain awnings (no text), slate road with cream lane dashes; hero in his sprite colours (brown skin, dark hair, grey glasses, white tee, black shorts, white/black trainers), opponent solid dark red, unbadged teal car, amber seatbelt.
2. **Shapes:** solid flat 2D fighters on a 14-joint rig — tapered limbs with round joints, soft round hands, rounded trainers, tee and shorts shapes; hair tufts, tee hem and shorts trail the motion (overlapping action); drawn at 2x and downsampled.
3. **Lighting:** flat fills with a near/far two-tone and a faint warm rim on the hero; one clean flat impact star on medium hits and ONE full-frame warm colour flash (4 frames) on the finishing hits and the crash — no rings, glows or speed lines.
4. **Camera / pacing:** fight shots ~0.5–0.7 s (reference median 0.60 s), wide shots with fighters >= 40 % of frame height and big close-ups, slow push-ins within shots; a follow move on his hips through the knock-back and whip pan to the car, ride-along, wide barrier crash, push-in to the locked belt at the freeze, slow push-in through the explainer, whip pan back to the street, settle to the opening wide for the loop.
5. **Finish:** 6 parallax layers (sky, far skyline, mid shopfronts, near lamp posts, road, soft foreground shapes) with per-layer depth blur, 4 sub-frame motion blur only on fast strikes / whip pans / the drive, static vignette.
