|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Since about the 2012 model year, the BMW M5 uses "Active Sound Design", which plays an engine sound through the car's audio speakers, generated from engine data (revs, load). It is controversial with fans. The engine sound is real; the speakers add to what the cabin hears. Said "Part of that comes through the speakers", "BMW calls it Active Sound Design. The engine is real. The volume is assisted." Prop: speaker icon "ACTIVE SOUND DESIGN". No year spoken. | Scenes 1–2 | Car and Driver, "Faking It: Engine-Sound Enhancement Explained" (2012); Motor Trend / Road & Track coverage of the F10 M5 (2012) |
| 2 | From mid-2022 BMW sold heated seats as a subscription in some countries (UK, Germany, South Korea, NZ, South Africa among them), not the US. Said "At the time, in some countries, those were a subscription." | Scene 3 | dpa via The Star; The Verge; Digital Trends (Jul 2022) — see bmw-heated-seats/FACTS.md |
| 3 | HEDGED, prices quoted at the time (South Korea store, converted): about $18 a month, $180 a year, about $415 unlimited. Said "About eighteen dollars a month", "a hundred and eighty a year", "about four-fifteen, unlimited", and in scene 7 "Eighteen dollars a month" (the running joke, same price). Tag "$18/month"; card "$18/month · $180/year · $415 unlimited". | Scenes 3, 4, 7 | The Verge; Edmunds; ArenaEV (Jul 2022) |
| 4 | If you stopped paying, the seat heating was switched off (the heaters stay fitted). Said "If you stop paying, the seats turn off", "The hardware is yours, sir. The warmth is rented." | Scenes 4–5 | dpa via The Star (2022) |
| 5 | BMW dropped the heated-seat subscription in 2023 (September). Not spoken; the "at the time" hedge covers it. | — | Carscoops; Edmunds (Sep 2023) |
| 6 | The BMW Z4 M40i (G29) and Toyota GR Supra (A90) share a platform, BMW's B58 3.0-litre turbocharged inline-six, the ZF 8-speed automatic, and are both built by Magna Steyr in Graz, Austria. Said "Three-litre inline-six, eight-speed automatic, built in Austria", "Same engine. Same gearbox. Same factory. Different badge." Chips "3.0L INLINE-SIX", "8-SPEED AUTO", "BUILT IN AUSTRIA"; "ENGINE / GEARBOX / FACTORY: SAME", "BADGE: DIFFERENT". | Scenes 5–6 | Car and Driver (2019 Z4 / Supra reviews); Toyota and BMW press releases (2018–19); Magna Steyr Graz plant (Wikipedia, "Magna Steyr") — see supra-z4/FACTS.md |
