# GR86 vs BRZ — fact sheet

Every claim spoken or shown, as used. Facts come from the session brief (2026-11-19); they were not re-checked against
primary sources in this session.

| Claim (as used) | Where | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Toyota and Subaru jointly developed the cars; both are built on the same line at Subaru's Gunma Main Plant, Ōta, Japan | Scenes 1–2 | Brief |
| The companies agreed to collaborate in October 2005 ("agreed to work together in 2005") | Scene 3 | Brief |
| Toyota raised its Subaru stake to 16.5% in April 2008 | Scene 3 | Brief |
| First generation production started March 2012 | Scene 3 (card) | Brief |
| Second generation: 2.4-litre naturally aspirated Subaru boxer ("No turbo"), rear-wheel drive, six-speed manual or automatic | Scene 4 | Brief |
| Differences are mostly badges, front fascias and tuning | Scene 5 | Brief |
| Curb weights within about 15–20 lb (GR86 about 2,811–2,868 lb, BRZ about 2,820–2,881 lb) | Scene 5 | Brief |

Softened: "front fascias" is spoken as "front bumpers" (card keeps "front fascias"); "within about 15–20 lb" is spoken
as "within about twenty pounds" and the card shows the ranges. "Built by Subaru" (hook stamp) reflects the shared line at
Subaru's plant; joint development is not denied. The stake is tied to 2008 only (no claim about today's share).

Photos: 2022 Toyota GR86 Premium (Elise240SX, CC BY-SA 4.0) and 2022 Subaru BRZ Limited (Mr.choppers, CC BY-SA 3.0 /
GFDL), both front-right three-quarter, Wikimedia Commons (`CREDITS.txt` next to the MP4). A car parked behind the GR86 was
removed from the cut-out. No spec is read from the photos. Background gags (two sheep on a filing cabinet, a penguin, a
knight mopping, a snail beating a tortoise to a finish flag, a marble bust that turns to watch, an octopus polishing the
platform) are jokes.
