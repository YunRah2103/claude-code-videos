01_toyota_corolla — script (Buyer: Gemini Fenrir, Salesman: Gemini Orus)


[Scene 1: showroom]
BUYER: You're telling me THIS is the best-selling car on Earth?!
SALESMAN: The Toyota Corolla. Yes.
BUYER: I expected a supercar! Something exciting!
SALESMAN: Supercars are rare. Corollas are everywhere.

[Scene 2: office]
BUYER: How many are we talking?
SALESMAN: Over fifty million. Since 1966.
BUYER: Fifty MILLION?!
SALESMAN: Toyota calls it the world's best-selling nameplate.

[Scene 3: gallery]
BUYER: But it's so boring!
SALESMAN: Twelve generations of boring.

[Scene 4: garage]
BUYER: Why does anyone even buy it?
SALESMAN: It's famous for reliability.
BUYER: That's it?!
SALESMAN: It's affordable. And cheap to run.

[Scene 5: factory]
BUYER: Nobody dreams about a Corolla!
SALESMAN: No. They just keep buying them.

[Scene 6: lot]
BUYER: So boring wins?
SALESMAN: Exciting or reliable? Tell me in the comments.
