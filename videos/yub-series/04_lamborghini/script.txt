04_lamborghini — script (Buyer: Gemini Fenrir, Salesman: Gemini Orus)


[Scene 1: showroom]
BUYER: Lamborghini started out making TRACTORS?!
SALESMAN: Ferruccio Lamborghini built tractors. From 1948.
BUYER: Then why make supercars?!
SALESMAN: He owned a Ferrari. And he wasn't happy.

[Scene 2: garage]
BUYER: What was wrong with it?
SALESMAN: The story goes, the clutch kept failing.
BUYER: So he fixed it himself?
SALESMAN: Reportedly, it was the same clutch as in his tractors.

[Scene 3: office]
BUYER: Then what happened?!
SALESMAN: The story goes, Enzo Ferrari told him to stick to tractors.
BUYER: He did NOT!
SALESMAN: Nobody can prove it. But Ferruccio built his own car.

[Scene 4: farm]
BUYER: A tractor guy versus Ferrari?!
SALESMAN: He already knew how to build machines.

[Scene 5: factory]
BUYER: When was this?!
SALESMAN: 1963. Automobili Lamborghini.

[Scene 6: gallery]
BUYER: And the tractors?
SALESMAN: Still made today. Under another owner.
SALESMAN: Legend or myth? Tell me in the comments.
