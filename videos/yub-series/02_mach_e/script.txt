02_mach_e — script (Buyer: Gemini Fenrir, Salesman: Gemini Orus)


[Scene 1: showroom]
BUYER: Who put a Mustang badge on an electric SUV?!
SALESMAN: Ford did. In 2019.
BUYER: A Mustang is a muscle car! It needs a roar!
SALESMAN: This one hums. It's the Mach-E.

[Scene 2: gallery]
BUYER: Mustangs have been two-door cars since 1964!
SALESMAN: And in 2019, the family grew. First time in 55 years.

[Scene 3: track]
BUYER: Fine. Is it at least fast?
SALESMAN: The GT Performance Edition makes 480 horsepower.
BUYER: And zero to sixty?
SALESMAN: Ford says 3.5 seconds.
BUYER: That's... actually quick.

[Scene 4: charging]
BUYER: But where's the engine roar?!
SALESMAN: Electric motors. No roar.

[Scene 5: office]
BUYER: Purists must hate this!
SALESMAN: Some did. Ford built it anyway.

[Scene 6: lot]
BUYER: So is it a real Mustang or not?
SALESMAN: Mustang or not? Tell me in the comments.
