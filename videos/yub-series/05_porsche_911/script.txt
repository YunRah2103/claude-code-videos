05_porsche_911 — script (Buyer: Gemini Fenrir, Salesman: Gemini Orus)


[Scene 1: showroom]
BUYER: Your engine is in the wrong place!
SALESMAN: Behind the rear axle. On purpose.
BUYER: Who builds a car like that?!
SALESMAN: Porsche. In the 911. Since 1963.

[Scene 2: gallery]
BUYER: Sixty years and nobody fixed it?!
SALESMAN: It debuted as the 901. Peugeot objected to the name.
BUYER: Focus! Why the back?!

[Scene 3: track]
SALESMAN: The engine's weight sits over the driven wheels. More traction.
BUYER: And braking?
SALESMAN: Porsche says it helps there too.

[Scene 4: garage]
BUYER: But doesn't the tail swing out?!
SALESMAN: Early ones had that reputation. Engineers kept taming it.

[Scene 5: office]
BUYER: Did they ever try to replace it?
SALESMAN: Reportedly, yes. With the front-engined 928.
BUYER: And?!
SALESMAN: The 928 is gone. The 911 is still here.

[Scene 6: lot]
BUYER: So it was never a mistake?
SALESMAN: Genius or mistake? Comment below.
