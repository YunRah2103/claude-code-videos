# Facts used in the style preview

**Claim on screen:** "DOUBLE THE SPEED?" → 100 → 200 km/h → "DRAG x4".

**Fact:** Doubling speed roughly quadruples aerodynamic drag.

- Drag force: F = ½ · ρ · C_d · A · v². With air density ρ, drag coefficient C_d and frontal area A held
  constant, F scales with v², so 2× the speed gives about 2² = 4× the drag force.
- "Roughly" because C_d changes a little with speed (Reynolds number), and wind, air density and ride height
  vary. The x4 is the textbook approximation, not a measured value for any real car.
- Related, not shown: the power needed to overcome drag scales with v³ (about 8× at double speed).
  The video does not claim this.
- Rolling resistance and other losses are not part of the "x4"; the readout is aerodynamic drag only.

The car is a generic procedural model (no brand, logo or emblem).
