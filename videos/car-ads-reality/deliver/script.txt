CAR ADS vs REALITY: script
BUYER = Fenrir (series voice). SALESMAN = Schedar (new for this episode).
Each claim scene (1-7) opens with a 3-second PARODY AD cutaway: original art, claim text only, plus a fine-print strip.


[SCENE 0] COLD OPEN: "Starting at" (Ford Maverick, showroom)
  BUYER: Starting at $28,145! I want that one!
  SALESMAN: Starting at. The two most expensive words in this building.
  BUYER: It's right there! In big letters!
  SALESMAN: That's the MSRP. Ford's own footnote says it excludes destination, taxes and fees.
  BUYER: Destination?! It's going to my driveway!
  SALESMAN: Your driveway charges a fee.
  BUYER: Fine. Fine! It's a little asterisk. I can live with a little asterisk.

[SCENE 1] "Up to" mpg (Toyota Corolla Hybrid, gas station)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Next! Toyota Corolla Hybrid! Up to 50 miles per gallon!
  SALESMAN: Up to. My second favorite phrase.
  BUYER: Fifty is fifty!
  SALESMAN: Fifty combined is the front-wheel-drive one. The EPA rates the SE with all-wheel drive at 44.
  BUYER: So the cool one gets six less?
  SALESMAN: The cool one gets six less.
  BUYER: Still a hybrid! Still happy! Next!

[SCENE 2] America's Best Warranty (Hyundai, showroom)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Hyundai! Ten years! A hundred thousand miles! America's Best Warranty!
  SALESMAN: For the original owner.
  BUYER: I am extremely original!
  SALESMAN: Sell it, and the next owner's powertrain cover drops to five years or 60,000 miles.
  BUYER: So the warranty doesn't like the next guy?
  SALESMAN: It's never met him.
  BUYER: Okay. Okay. That's actually fair. I'm calm. I'm calm.

[SCENE 3] 0-60 in 1.99 s (Tesla Model S Plaid, drag strip)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Tesla Model S Plaid! Zero to sixty in 1.99 seconds!
  SALESMAN: With one foot of rollout subtracted.
  BUYER: Subtracted from WHAT?!
  SALESMAN: The clock starts after the car has already rolled one foot.
  BUYER: So zero isn't zero?!
  SALESMAN: MotorTrend timed it from a true standstill. 2.28.
  BUYER: That's still insane!
  SALESMAN: It's insanely quick. Just not 1.99 from zero.
  BUYER: Who starts a race one foot in?!
  SALESMAN: Drag strips. It's an old convention.

[SCENE 4] Max towing (Ford F-150, tow lot)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Ford F-150! 13,500 pounds of towing!
  SALESMAN: When properly equipped.
  BUYER: I will equip it SO properly!
  SALESMAN: You need the 3.5 EcoBoost, the Tow Haul Package, and the optional Max Tow Axle.
  BUYER: And with the standard engine?
  SALESMAN: Ford's towing guide says 8,400. At most.
  BUYER: That's five thousand pounds of difference! That's a whole other boat!
  SALESMAN: Please don't tow a whole other boat.

[SCENE 5] EPA range (F-150 Lightning, highway charger)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Fine! Electric! F-150 Lightning! 320 miles of range!
  SALESMAN: That's the EPA estimate.
  BUYER: So?!
  SALESMAN: Consumer Reports drove one at a steady 70 miles per hour. It ran out at 270.
  BUYER: FIFTY MILES! Where did fifty miles GO?!
  SALESMAN: The EPA test mixes city and highway driving. At highway speed, EVs use more energy.
  BUYER: And the other cars?!
  SALESMAN: Of 22 EVs they tested, nearly half fell short. Some went farther.
  BUYER: So my range is a coin toss?!

[SCENE 6] Fratzonic exhaust (Dodge Charger Daytona, sound stage)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: At least THIS one is honest. Dodge Charger Daytona! Listen to that exhaust!
  SALESMAN: It's electric.
  BUYER: Then what is THAT?!
  SALESMAN: Dodge calls it the Fratzonic Chambered Exhaust. An amplifier and a tuning chamber at the back.
  BUYER: It's a SPEAKER?!
  SALESMAN: Up to 126 decibels. Dodge says that matches a Hellcat.
  BUYER: My car is doing an impression of a car?!
  SALESMAN: There's also a Stealth Mode.
  BUYER: Of course there is.

[SCENE 7] "Full Self-Driving" (Tesla, test track)
  (PARODY AD cutaway: claim text + asterisk; jingle)
  BUYER: Last one. Tesla. Full. Self. Driving!
  SALESMAN: Supervised.
  BUYER: What?
  SALESMAN: The full name is Full Self-Driving, Supervised.
  BUYER: Supervised by WHO?!
  SALESMAN: You. Tesla says it requires active driver supervision and does not make the car autonomous.
  BUYER: So I drive while it drives?!
  SALESMAN: The manual says keep your hands on the wheel and pay attention at all times.
  BUYER: Then what is the SELF doing?!
  SALESMAN: Driving. Supervised.

[SCENE 8] ENDING (showroom)
  BUYER: I've decided.
  SALESMAN: Yes?
  BUYER: From now on, I read the asterisk first.
  SALESMAN: The asterisk has fine print too.
  BUYER: Then I'm buying a bicycle.
  SALESMAN: Pedals sold separately.
  SALESMAN: Which claim annoyed you the most? Tell us in the comments.

[END CARD] "Which claim annoyed you the most? Tell us in the comments*"  (*comments subject to fine print)
