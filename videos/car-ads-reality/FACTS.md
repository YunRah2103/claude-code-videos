# Car Ads vs Reality: fact sheet

Every claim below is phrased as "the ad says X, the spec sheet says Y". No accusations, no invented numbers.
Checked 2026-11-21. Scenes go from mildest to most outrageous.

## Dropped claims and why
- **"Best-in-class towing"**: ford.com uses "Best-in-Class" only for the F-150's *payload* (2,430 lbs); towing is "max available". The scene uses Ford's real wording instead.
- **Exact Maverick destination fee**: sources disagree ($1,695 on Cars.com/TFL vs $1,845 on Edmunds and owner forums). We say "excludes destination" with no number.
- **Car and Driver / SAE 2025 "EVs 12.5% short at 75 mph"**: I couldn't open the primary article, so the scene uses Consumer Reports only.
- **Cold-weather range loss**: I couldn't fetch a primary test dataset with model-specific numbers, so it was left out.
