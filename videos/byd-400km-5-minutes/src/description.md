# BYD: 400 km in 5 minutes?

BYD says its new megawatt charger can add about 400 kilometres in five minutes. That's 1,000 kilowatts, twice the peak power of Tesla's latest Supercharger, according to BYD. Claimed, of course. But the race to charge faster is on. Follow for the next one.

Figures are as stated by the manufacturer where the voiceover says "says" or "claims".
