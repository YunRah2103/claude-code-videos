# GR Yaris: three cylinders, 257 hp

The Toyota GR Yaris has a 1.6-litre, three-cylinder turbo engine, making 257 horsepower. Its all-wheel drive sends 60 percent to the front by default. Switch to Sport, and 70 percent goes to the rear. Track mode splits it evenly. Follow for the next one.

Figures are as stated by the manufacturer where the voiceover says "says" or "claims".
