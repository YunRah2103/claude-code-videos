Photos: 2022 Mitsubishi Outlander SE Launch Edition (Gold Pony) and 2021 Nissan Rogue (North Island Nissan), both
YouTube CC BY via Wikimedia Commons (`CREDITS.txt` next to the MP4). They are front three-quarter shots from opposite
sides, so the pair always stands facing each other; the Rogue photo is cropped at its rear by the original frame and is
faded out at the far left. No spec is read from the photos. Background gags (a llama taking selfies, a dinosaur costume
sipping tea, a giraffe through the ceiling, a bear in a bathrobe reading the paper, a vending machine that dispenses a
chicken, a banana costume with a coffee) are jokes.
